# Nintendo 64 (NUS-001, charcoal) + Game Pak: geometric reference

Research only. No 3D model files were downloaded or copied. Numbers come from published specs, the two Nintendo
design patents (orthographic drawings measured with pixel scans and scaled to the published overall size), and
Wikimedia Commons photos measured the same way.

**Confidence key:** **H** = published spec or several sources agree. **M** = measured from an orthographic patent
drawing scaled to a known dimension (about ±2–3 mm). **L** = estimated from perspective photos (about ±5 mm or
worse), or inferred.

**Coordinate system (console):** origin at the centre of the 260 × 190 plan bounding box, on the table plane.
+x points right (seen from the front), +z points to the front, +y points up (y = 0 is the table). So the rear
edge is at z = −95, the front at z = +95, and the outer sides at x = ±130.

**Important design facts (some contradict the brief's assumptions):**
- The cartridge hump and slot are at the **rear** of the top surface, not the centre. The front half of the top
  has the POWER and RESET sliders and the Jumper Pak lid.
- The plan view is **wider at the front**, not the back. The rear body is about 226 mm wide. Two round "pod"
  bumpers at the front corners bring the width to 260 mm.
- The cartridge does **not** have a narrower connector neck. It is the same width all the way down, with an arched
  top. There are three vertical bands on its face, and step notches at the bottom corners.

Primary geometry sources:
- **P-console:** US Des. 379,832, "Game machine", K. Ashida, filed 24 May 1995, granted 10 Jun 1997.
  Figures: FIG 3 front, FIG 4 top plan, FIG 5 rear, FIG 6 bottom, FIG 7/8 sides. Sheets:
  https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/pages/USD379832-2.png, -3.png, -4.png.
  This is a pre-production drawing. The front, top and plan outline match retail units. **The rear panel and
  side-vent placement differ from retail**, so use the photos for those.
- **P-cart:** US Des. 376,795, "Game cartridge", granted 24 Dec 1996. Figures: FIG 3 bottom, FIG 4 front,
  FIG 5 top, FIG 6 side, FIG 7 back. Sheets:
  https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/pages/USD376795-1.png, -2.png, -3.png.
- **WP:** Wikipedia "Nintendo 64" and "Nintendo 64 Game Pak".
- **D.com:** dimensions.com/element/nintendo-64.
- **N64E:** nintendo64ever.com technical specifications.
- **Photos:** Evan-Amos Commons photos (FL/FR/BL/BR/Bottom/Set) and others, listed in §6.

## 1. Console overall shape

### 1.1 Overall size

| Dimension | Value | Conf. | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Width (x) | **260 mm** (259.8) | H | WP, D.com, N64E |
| Depth (z) | **190 mm** | H | WP, D.com, N64E |
| Height (y), to the top of the hump at the slot rim | **73 mm** (72.9) | H | WP, D.com, N64E |
| Mass | 1.1 kg | H | WP, N64E |

**Conflicting values:**
- The Science Museum Group catalogue gives 70 × 260 × 180 mm. This is probably a rounded or alternative
  measurement; ignore it.
- Patent FIG 3 has height/width proportions that give about 77 mm. The pre-production drawing is roughly 5% taller.
  All heights below were rescaled so the total is 73 mm.

### 1.2 Plan-view outline (top view, from P-console FIG 4 and FIG 6, scaled to 260 × 190)

Build it as the union of a tapered rear body and two front corner discs, joined by a concave flare.

| Element | Value | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Rear edge | Straight, z = −95, width **226 mm** (x ±113) | M |
| Rear plan corners | Vertical-axis radius **R ≈ 25 mm** | M |
| Side walls | Taper slightly outward going forward: x ±113 at z = −72, to about x ±117 at z ≈ +6 (≈ 3° per side) | M |
| Front corner pods | Cylinders, plan **radius ≈ 26.5 mm (Ø 53)**, centre x = ±103.5, z ≈ +62 (±5). Outermost points at x = ±130, z ≈ +57 | M |
| Side-to-pod flare | Concave fillet R ≈ 30–40 mm, from z ≈ +6 into the pods | M/L |
| Front face between the pods | Slightly convex. About z = +88 at x = ±40, +90 at centre. The port bezels stand proud to z ≈ +93…+95, so the bezels set the 190 mm depth | M |

### 1.3 Height profile (y), assembled from P-console FIG 3/7/8 (rescaled to 73) and photos

| Element | Value | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Feet | 0 → ~5 mm. Rear: dome feet. Front: large discs under the pods (see §3.9) | M |
| Lower shell ("skirt") | y ≈ 5 → 31 mm. Vertical walls with a small draft | M |
| Horizontal parting seam | Front: y ≈ 31 mm, running through the centres of the controller ports. Sides: y ≈ 30–37 mm, rising slightly toward the rear | M |
| Front pods | Shell from y ≈ 11 to y ≈ 43. Below the shell, a recessed black base disc from y = 0 to 11. Top of the pod is a rounded dome-cylinder (top-edge fillet R ≈ 10–12) and sits **below** the main deck: the deck overhangs and steps back above the pods | M |
| Main deck (upper shell) | Sides and front corners: top surface at y ≈ **58–60 mm**, nearly flat with a slight crown | M |
| Deck perimeter fillet | R ≈ 8–10 mm | L |
| Front "nose" | Centre front rises to about y ≈ 65 mm at the top of the logo plate, then falls forward in a sloped face that carries the logo plate. The front face leans back about 20–30° from vertical between y ≈ 35 and 65 | M/L |
| Cartridge hump, plan | U/bell shape that runs off the rear edge. Width at the rear edge 143 mm (x ±71.5). Narrows to x ±59 at z ≈ −26. Front end is a semi-ellipse (semi-axes 59 in x, ~40 in z) whose front point is at z ≈ +14 | M |
| Cartridge hump, height | Rises from the deck (y ≈ 59) to **y = 73** at the slot, a raise of about 14 mm. The top is flattish around the slot. The front slope (which carries the vent grille) blends into the deck with a large radius, R ≈ 40–60 mm | M |
| Hump side and rear | Side flanks blend with R ≈ 15–20. At the rear the hump meets the rear wall with R ≈ 8 | L |
| Side profile | The tallest point is at the rear (hump). From about z = −20 the hump slopes down to the deck height at z ≈ +25. The deck stays about flat (y 57–59) to the rounded front edge | M |
| Side-wall crease | A shallow diagonal crease on each side wall, running from low-rear (y ≈ 30, z ≈ −90) to higher-front (y ≈ 37, z ≈ +10) | L |

**Modelling recipe (suggested):**
1. Extrude the rear-body plan outline to y = 59. Fillet the top edge R 9.
2. Add the two pod cylinders (Ø 53) from y = 11 to y = 43. Round their top edge R 10. Under each pod, add a
   black disc Ø 44 from y = 0 to 11.
3. Boolean-union a lofted U-shaped hump (from y = 59 at its outline to y = 73 on its top) and add a large blend
   to the deck.
4. Sculpt the nose bulge at the front centre (up to y ≈ 65 at x = 0, fading out by x = ±35).
5. Cut the parting seam: 0.8 mm groove at y = 31.

| Feature | Value | Conf. | Source |
|---|---|---|---|

| "Nintendo" embossed oval | On the hump top just **behind** the slot. Centre x = 0, z ≈ −78. Size about 40 × 8 mm, raised about 0.5 mm. Position only, no text | L | Photos N64-rear, BL, NUS-006 rear |

### 3.4 Controller port detail (front view)

These are the retail parts as seen in the Evan-Amos FL photo, measured at full resolution.

- **Bezel:** a light-grey ring, almost round (Ø ≈ 22 mm, maybe 1 mm taller than wide). It stands about 2 mm proud
  of the charcoal front face and has a slightly dished face. The parting seam meets the bezel at its mid-height.
- **Socket mouth:** a **rounded trapezoid, wider at the top**, cut about 6 mm into the bezel.
  - Top edge straight, about **13.5 mm**.
  - Bottom edge about **10 mm**.
  - Height about **7.5 mm**.
  - Lower corners R ≈ 2.5, upper corners R ≈ 1.
  - The cavity wall shows as a dark ring. The floor/insert is light grey with **three round holes, Ø ≈ 2.5 mm,
    pitch ≈ 3.8 mm**, in a horizontal row at mid-height.
  - Socket centre about 1.5 mm above the bezel centre.
- **Port-number dots:** 1, 2, 3 or 4 small dots (Ø ≈ 1 mm) on the bezel below the socket. Optional detail.
- **Spacing:**
  - Within a pair: about **30 mm**, centre to centre.
  - Gap between the inner ports: about **72 mm**. The logo plate and LED sit in this gap.
  - From the outer ports to the pod flank: about 20 mm.

### 3.8 Rear panel detail (retail)

The patent's FIG 5 does not match retail. These values come from photos N64-rear.jpg, Nintendo-64-Console-BL/BR
and the NUS-006 rear view.

- **Power-supply bay:** a large rectangular opening in the lower rear wall. About **125 mm wide × 38 mm tall**
  (y ≈ 3 … 41), centre x ≈ −10 (biased toward the console's left side), about 55 mm deep.
  - The floor inside has ribs.
  - A 2 × 3-pin power connector sits at the inner right end (seen from behind), i.e. toward −x.
  - The detachable PSU brick (NUS-002) slides in and sticks out about 30–35 mm behind the console.
  - There is no separate DC jack.
- **AV Multi-Out:** a small rectangular port, about 22 × 9 mm, in a recessed pocket of about 40 × 20 mm. Low on the
  rear wall at the console's right side as seen from the front: x ≈ +95, y ≈ 18.
- **Service sticker:** a ~70 × 22 mm area on the rear wall at about x ≈ +80, y ≈ 45. US units carry a sticker
  here; it is optional.
- **Rear wall:** vertical. The upper shell overhangs it slightly. The parting line runs at y ≈ 30–35.

## 5. Game Pak (cartridge)

**Coordinate system (cart):** x across the width, y up from the bottom (connector) edge, z the thickness; the front
(label side) faces the console's front when inserted.

### 5.1 Overall size

| Dimension | Value | Conf. | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Width | **116 mm** | H | WP Game Pak ("11.6 cm wide"); Total Apex Gaming (116 mm) |
| Height (at centre, top of the arch) | **76.6 mm** (WP) / **78.2 mm** (P-cart proportions scaled to 116 wide) | M | WP vs P-cart FIG 4 and FIG 7, which agree with each other |
| Thickness | **18.5 mm** | H/M | WP. P-cart FIG 6 gives 18.0 and FIG 5 gives 19.2 |

**Recommendation:** model 116 × 77 × 18.5 (split the height difference).

**Conflicts:** iFixit gives 117 × 89 × 20 mm. The 89 mm looks wrong, as no other source comes close.

### 5.2 Outline (front view), from P-cart FIG 4, scaled to 116 wide

- **Width:** constant 116 mm from the bottom to the shoulders. **There is no narrow connector neck.**
- **Top edge:** one shallow circular arc, **R ≈ 146 mm**. It drops about **12 mm** from the centre to the outer
  edges, so the side walls are about 65 mm tall.
- **Shoulders:** the outer top corners are rounded, R ≈ 3. All silhouette edges have about R 1.5–2.
- **Bottom edge:** straight. All four bottom corners are sharp-ish (R ≈ 1).

### 5.3 Front face (label side)

- **Three vertical bands.**
  - A central band **68.7 mm** wide (x ±34.3).
  - Two side bands, about **22.7 mm** each.
  - The bands are separated by narrow V-grooves, about 1 mm wide and 0.5 mm deep, running the full height.
- **Label recess:**
  - Size: **55.7 mm wide × 65.5 mm tall**, centred in x.
  - Position: bottom edge at y = **6.4**; top edge about 6.2 mm below the top-centre of the arch, following the
    arch curve.
  - Corners R ≈ 4, depth about 0.5–0.8 mm.
  - Confidence: M.
- **Bottom-outer-corner step notches (one each side):**
  - The outer part of each side band is stepped back in the lower region.
  - About **9.5 mm wide** (from the outer edge inward) × **28 mm tall** (from the bottom).
  - Step depth about 3–4 mm (L). The top end of the step is angled/chamfered.
  - Confidence: M for the outline, L for the depth.
- **Grip ridges:** **none**. There are no ribbed grips. The only relief is the band grooves and the corner steps.
  The side walls (thickness faces) are plain, apart from a short curved crease near the bottom that comes from the
  step notch (FIG 6).

### 5.4 Back face (P-cart FIG 7; photo MajorasMaskBack.jpg)

- **Bands:** the same three-band layout. The central band is about 71.6 mm wide and the side bands about 22.2 mm
  each. A thin raised rim follows the arch across the top of the central band.
- **Back label recess (sticker):**
  - Size: **80 mm wide × 40 mm tall**, R ≈ 3.
  - Position: bottom at y ≈ **13.9**, top at y ≈ 53.7 (24.6 mm below the top-centre). Centred.
  - Confidence: M.
- **Two security screws:** x ≈ **±32**, about 10 mm below the top-centre (y ≈ 67). Heads Ø ≈ 3.8 mm (Gamebit).
  Confidence: L (photo).
- **Embossed "Nintendo" oval:** centre x = 0, y ≈ 70, size ≈ 28 × 6 mm, raised. Position only, no text.
  Confidence: L.
- **Two rectangular notches on the bottom edge:**
  - Each about **10.3 mm wide × 10.8 mm tall**, about 3 mm deep.
  - Located **8.5 … 18.8 mm** in from each outer edge.
  - These are the "two small indentations" that engage the dust flaps (WP).
  - Confidence: M.

### 5.5 Bottom face

- A long opening exposes the PCB edge connector: about **71 mm** wide (the central-band width), about 6 mm in the
  thickness direction, with the gold fingers about 3 mm inside (P-cart FIG 3).
- Confidence: M.

### 5.6 Inserted position

- **Orientation:** the label faces the front of the console. The cart stands vertically in the slot, at x = 0,
  z ≈ −51.
- **Insertion depth:** about **14–20 mm** below the slot rim. Confidence: **L**.
  - In the rear photo (Commons, NUS-006 rear), the bottom edge of the back label (13.9 mm above the cart bottom)
    sits right at the rim.
  - In the front photo (Commons 7973388038), the whole front label is visible above the slot.
- **Protrusion above the rim:** about **58–64 mm**, so the cart top is at about **y ≈ 131–137** above the table.
  Confidence: L.
- No published spec for this was found. Check it against the photos in §6 before the final animation.

## 7. Gaps and open items

- **Slider and lid heights:** the exact heights of the slider knobs and Jumper lid above the deck (estimated
  4–6 mm, flush) were not found.
- **Grille geometry:** the exact grille slit count and arc radius on retail units. The patent shows 21 straight
  slits; retail units are curved. Count them on FL at full resolution if it matters.
- **Rear panel:** the exact PSU-bay and AV-port sizes come only from perspective photos (L).
- **Side vents:** the count and placement on retail units (L).
- **Cartridge insertion:** the insertion depth and protrusion are inferred (L). No spec was found.
- **Cartridge height:** 76.6 (WP) vs 78.2 (patent proportions).
- **Step notch depth:** the depth of the cart's front step notches (L).
- **Colours:** derived from photos. No official Pantone or RAL values exist publicly.
- **Measured shells:** ConsoleMods' "Dimensions for Game Cartridges" page and the Printables "N64 Cartridge Shell
  Redux" (caliper-measured) could not be fetched (403). They may hold caliper values for the cart.
